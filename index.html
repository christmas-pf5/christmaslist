Evergreen Christmas World

Money towards a mini Christmas village 🎄

Another lovely option is a contribution towards this miniature Christmas village project. Here’s the materials checklist from the project plan, so you can see what it’s all for.

Ultimate shopping & materials checklist

01 · Base

  • 80 × 60 cm tray / board
  • Extra foam board for height
  • Brown / white paper

02 · Building materials

  • 8–10 cereal boxes
  • Thick cardstock — cream, red, evergreen
  • Corrugated cardboard
  • Scrap paper for roof tiles, doors and windows

03 · Town centre

  • Central Christmas tree — green cardstock or bottle brush
  • 3 market stalls — lolly sticks, card roofs
  • 2 benches — lolly sticks, brown paint
  • 4 lanterns — cocktail sticks, beads, black + gold paint
  • Town sign / noticeboard
  • Winding white paths — cream paper
  • Tiny pebbles

04 · Scenery

  • Snow — white paper, cotton or fake scatter
  • 15–20 evergreen bottle-brush trees
  • Tiny fences & gates — lolly sticks, white paint
  • Snowy hills — newspaper + white paper
  • Secret woodland cabin with letterbox + sign

05 · Decorating

  • Pencils, ruler, eraser, scissors, glue stick, PVA, hot glue gun
  • Pens — red, green, cream, brown, gold, black
  • Paint — evergreen, cranberry, cream, gold, white, brown, black
  • Brushes
  • Twine, ribbon, buttons, gift wrap

06 · Tiny details

  • Wooden beads, gold + red ornaments
  • Tiny paper stars — gold
  • Tiny wreaths — pipe cleaner
  • Battery fairy lights — warm white, copper wire, 2 m

07 · Paperwork archive

  • Small folder with cream pages
  • Shop signs: Sugarplum Bakery, Toy Cupboard, North Pole Post, Holly & Berry, Gingerbread, Golden Star, Evergreen Express, Village Green
  • Bakery menu, toy catalogue, envelopes, postcards, Gazette, calendar and resident cards for Mabel, Oliver, Edith, Mr Finch and Mayor Clementine

08 · Safety

  • Adult help for knives, hot glue, electrics
Thank you! A contribution of any size towards the mini Christmas village would be appreciated just as much as choosing a gift from the list.
